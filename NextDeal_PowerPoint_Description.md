### **What is NextDeal?**
A comprehensive automotive marketplace platform that revolutionizes vehicle transactions through an intuitive, feature-rich web application connecting car buyers, sellers, dealers, and automotive service providers.

### **Key Features**

**🏠 For Users:**
- Advanced vehicle marketplace with smart filtering
- Hot vehicles & rental marketplace
- Certified garages & recommended agencies
- Interactive community & real-time chat
- User profiles & favorites system

**🏢 For Dealers:**
- Complete inventory management
- Lead management & analytics dashboard
- Advertisement tools & bulk messaging
- Customer relationship management

**🔧 For Admins:**
- User & dealer management
- Content moderation & analytics
- Campaign & subscription management
- CRM integration & email logs

## 🛠️ **SLIDE 2: TECHNOLOGY & BUSINESS IMPACT**

### **Modern Technology Stack**

**Frontend:**
- **Next.js 15.2.3** with App Router & Turbopack
- **React 19.0.0** with TypeScript
- **Material-UI v6.4.8** + **Tailwind CSS v4**
- **Zustand** state management + **TanStack Query**

**Key Technologies:**
- **JWT Authentication** with role-based access
- **Socket.IO** for real-time chat
- **i18next** (English/Hebrew with RTL support)
- **Formik + Yup** form handling
- **CKEditor 5** rich text editing
- **Recharts** data visualization

**Architecture:**
- Component-based modular design
- 80+ reusable components
- 12+ API endpoints
- Responsive mobile-first design
- Docker containerization ready
